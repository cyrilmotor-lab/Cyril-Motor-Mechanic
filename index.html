ABOUT CYRIL MOTOR MECHANIC

OUR CUSTOMERS ARE FAMILY

Our customers are family, and family always takes care of each other.

Cyril Motor Mechanic is a trusted Australian automotive business with 17 years of experience in the automotive services industry.

We started as a small, local, family-owned business and have grown into a workshop proud to provide a high standard of service, specialising in car servicing, brakes, suspension, exhausts and auto-electrical work.

We pride ourselves on quality, trust and attention to our customers' needs. We offer down-to-earth, competitive pricing with no hidden surprises, and we are committed to expanding our services whenever we can to create a better and more transparent experience for our customers.

HOW WE OPERATE

BUILT AROUND COMMUNITY & TRUST

We operate as part of a community made up of customers, employees and suppliers. We work together in an open environment based on common goals, mutual trust and respect.

Our suppliers are treated as true partners in the running of the business. Our employees are trusted to take ownership, make decisions and contribute new ideas. We encourage an informal, enjoyable workplace where people can develop their skills and continuously improve the way we operate.

SAFETY FIRST

YOUR SAFETY MATTERS TO US

We take our customers' safety very seriously. Cyril Motor Mechanic employs qualified individuals to work on our customers' vehicles and uses quality products selected to meet appropriate factory-original specifications.

Our maintenance and repair work is performed by qualified technicians and overseen by management, with a strong focus on quality workmanship, reliable components and the safety of every vehicle that leaves our workshop.
