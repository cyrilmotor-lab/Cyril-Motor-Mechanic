Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 September 2026

Cyril Motor Mechanic ("we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use and protect personal information when you use our website, booking system or customer app.

Information We Collect

When you make a booking, we may collect your name, mobile phone number, email address, vehicle registration, vehicle details, preferred booking date and time, and information you provide about your vehicle or required service.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to manage bookings, communicate with customers, provide mechanical services, maintain vehicle service records, send booking confirmations and service-related reminders, and improve our services.

SMS and Communications

If you provide your mobile number when making a booking, we may use it to send service-related messages such as booking confirmations, appointment updates and reminders.

Google Services

Our booking and business systems may use Google services, including Google Calendar, to manage workshop appointments. Information shared with these services is used only for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy.

Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse or disclosure. Information may be stored using secure third-party technology providers that support our business systems.

Sharing Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with service providers where reasonably necessary to operate our booking, communication and business systems, or where required by law.

Access and Correction

You may contact us to request access to or correction of personal information we hold about you.
