ECU Tuning & Remapping

Cyril Motor Mechanic provides ECU diagnostics and calibration services for cars and 4WDs. Our approach is to understand the vehicle, diagnose existing issues and then select an appropriate calibration strategy.

01. Diagnose

Check fault codes and vehicle operating information before tuning work begins.

02. Assess

Consider the engine, transmission, hardware and the owner's intended use.

03. Calibrate

Apply a performance-focused calibration suited to the vehicle and requested outcome.

04. Verify

Check drivability and vehicle behaviour after the calibration process.
